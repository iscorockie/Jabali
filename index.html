The Jabali Field Standard

Permit pricing with nothing hidden.

Mountain gorilla permits are strictly regulated by the Uganda Wildlife Authority — capped at 8 visitors per habituated family per day. Unlike operators who hide permit markups inside opaque package rates, Jabali Trails passes 100% of the official UWA permit fee straight through at face value and locks your exact trekking sector (Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga, or Nkuringo) to match your lodge.
